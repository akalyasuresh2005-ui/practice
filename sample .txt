Chandrasekar was born in Thangachimadam, Tamil Nadu into a Catholic Christian family as S. A. Chandrasekaran.[4][5][6] His son is Joseph Vijay, who is a former actor and politician and currently serves as the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. Chandrasekhar also had a daughter named Vidya, who died at the age of two.[7] His brother-in-law S. N. Surendar is a playback singer and voice artist. His nephew Vikranth is also an actor,[8] and his grandson Jason Sanjay is a director who made his debut with Sigma (2026status
